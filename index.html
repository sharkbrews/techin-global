METHODOLOGY

The Techin Precision Overhaul Protocol

Six disciplined engineering stages from teardown to computerized load verification, ensuring certified OEM performance.

01

Inquiry & Intake

Job logging, visual fault inspection, barcode asset tagging, and baseline contamination assessment.

02

Precision Teardown

Controlled disassembly using hydraulic pullers and thorough multi-stage ultrasonic degreasing.

03

Metrology & NDT

Micrometer bore gauging, magnetic particle crack detection, and detailed wear analysis report.

04

Machining & Assembly

Barrel honing, hard chrome rod plating, micron valve lapping, and OEM seal replacement in clean bay.

05

Hydrostatic Test

Full working pressure testing up to 700 Bar on dynamic test bench with volumetric leak checks.

06

Certified Dispatch

Conformity certificate, serialized test report, protective port capping, and rapid logistics dispatch.
