## About the Company
Techin Global Hydraulics and Engineering specializes in the **repair, servicing, overhauling, and maintenance of industrial hydraulic and mechanical equipment**. They serve heavy industries, manufacturing plants, and infrastructure sectors — with a **special focus on equipment used in Indian Railways** (workshops, loco sheds, coach depots, and wagon repair units).

- Hydraulic power packs / power units

## Design & Visual Direction
- **Tone:** Industrial, trustworthy, precise, powerful — not playful or cartoonish. Think "heavy engineering meets modern SaaS design."
- **Color palette:** Deep steel blue / charcoal / graphite as the base, with a bold accent color like safety orange, amber, or electric yellow (common in industrial/engineering branding) for CTAs and highlights. Clean whites/light greys for content sections to keep it readable.
- **Typography:** Strong, modern sans-serif for headings (bold, slightly condensed — feels "engineered"), clean readable sans-serif for body text.
- **Animations:**
  - Smooth scroll-triggered fade/slide-in animations for each section
  - Subtle hover effects on service cards (lift + glow/shadow)
  - Animated counters for stats (e.g., "500+ Machines Serviced", "15+ Years Experience", "50+ Railway Workshops Served")
  - A hero section with a subtle looping animation or parallax effect (gears turning, hydraulic piston motion, or an animated line-drawing of machinery — tasteful, not gimmicky)
  - Smooth page transitions and a sticky navbar that shrinks/changes style on scroll
